# Content strategy

Demo strategy for the public corpus. It is not a personal editorial calendar.

Lanes used by the checker: POSITIONING, TREND_REACTION, PROJECT_SHOWCASE,
TECHNICAL_DEEP_DIVE, CAREER_ENGINEERING.

Audiences: BROAD_TECH, AI_ENGINEERING, SPECIALIST.

A topic needs a reason to write it now, a reason this author can say it,
and a reason a reader should care. Those fields are editorial metadata.
They are not proof.
